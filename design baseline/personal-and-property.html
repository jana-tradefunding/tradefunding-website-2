FAQ

Common questions

Can I get a home loan if I'm self-employed?

Yes. We work with lenders who specialise in low-doc and alt-doc lending, using tools like BAS, accountant declarations, or bank statements instead of two years of tax returns.

Will comparing affect my credit score?

No. We compare your situation against lender policy first. A credit check is only run once you choose to proceed with a specific lender.

What's the difference between low-doc and alt-doc?

Low-doc typically means reduced income verification (e.g. a signed declaration), while alt-doc means alternative evidence of income (like BAS or bank statements) rather than full financials.
