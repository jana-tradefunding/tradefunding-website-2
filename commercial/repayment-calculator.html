Frequently Asked Questions

Business loan repayments are calculated using an amortisation formula: M = P × [r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n − 1], where P is the principal (loan amount), r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments. Weekly and fortnightly figures are derived from the monthly repayment: weekly = monthly × 12 / 52, fortnightly = monthly × 12 / 26.

Three main factors determine your repayment amount: the loan principal (how much you borrow), the interest rate (which depends on your credit profile, security offered, and lender), and the loan term (longer terms mean lower repayments but more total interest paid). Your free Compare Report from Trade Funding will show you the exact rates and terms available to your business from 70+ lenders.

Choosing weekly or fortnightly repayments can save you money over the life of the loan because you effectively make slightly more payments per year, reducing the principal faster. Weekly repayments also align better with businesses that receive revenue weekly. However, monthly repayments are simpler to manage and align with most accounting cycles. The best choice depends on your cash flow pattern.
