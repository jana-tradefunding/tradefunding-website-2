The Problem

Business lending in Australia is broken

The market has more lenders, more products, and more complexity than ever. Yet businesses still make funding decisions with incomplete information.

1

Too many options, no way to compare

Dozens of lenders entered the Australian SME market. No transparent way exists for business owners to see them all side by side.

2

Wrong product, wrong lender

Too many businesses end up with products that don't match their actual purpose of funds. That costs them time, money, and opportunity.

3

Paying more than they should

Business owners paying far more in interest and fees than necessary, simply because they didn't know better options existed for their profile.

4

Larger businesses left underserved

Companies with genuine capital needs get offered amounts that don't solve the real problem, because the lender only has one product.

5

Security mismanagement

Businesses accessing funds without understanding how their security is used or the impact on their PPSR. The result: locking themselves out of future options.

6

Apply first, find out later

The standard process: apply to a lender, wait, get approved or declined, repeat. No visibility into what you'd actually qualify for until after you've committed.

Our Platform

Powered by Cashper. AI-driven pre-qualification across 70+ lenders.

Cashper is our proprietary lending intelligence engine. It doesn't just compare. It pre-qualifies your business against each lender's actual criteria and delivers personalised indicative offers, scored using our S.T.A.R. framework.

1

Lending Profile

Cashper absorbs your business data and builds a full lending profile: revenue, industry, trading history, security position.

2

Provider Matrix

Your profile is matched against 70+ lenders AND products based on your purpose of funds and business characteristics.

3

Credit Assessment

Individual credit engines, one for each lender on our panel, assess your creditworthiness against that lender's specific criteria.

4

Indicative Offers

Each engine produces personalised indicative terms. Not just "you might qualify", but actual estimated pricing and structure.

5

S.T.A.R. Scoring

Offers are scored on Security, Term, Amount, and Rate: the black and white facts about what each lender is offering and what they want in return.

6

Guided Execution

Our team walks you through the report, helps you choose, and runs the loan from application through to settlement.
