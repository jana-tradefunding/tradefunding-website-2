Advantages & Considerations

The honest assessment

Overall fit
Good fit for property-secured borrowing
Fast access to capital
Settlement in days not weeks, compared to 3–6 weeks for a full refinance.
Advantage
Higher interest rates than first mortgages
Rates typically 11%–18% p.a., significantly higher than first mortgage rates due to subordinate position.
Consideration
Keep your existing first mortgage rate
No need to refinance. Your current first mortgage stays untouched and you avoid break costs.
Advantage
Shorter loan terms
Typically 3–12 months, so you need a clear exit strategy or refinance plan.
Consideration
Short-term flexible arrangements
Interest-only or balloon repayment structures tailored to your needs and exit plan.
Advantage
Property valuation required
Most lenders require a property valuation, which may involve additional upfront costs.
Consideration
Multiple security types accepted
Residential, commercial, and rural property all accepted by specialist lenders.
Advantage
May have higher fees than traditional lending
Establishment fees, valuation costs, and legal fees can add up. Compare the total cost carefully.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A second mortgage is a loan secured against property you already have a first mortgage on. The second lender takes a subordinate charge, meaning they’re repaid after the first mortgage lender.
Specialist lenders like Arc, Aquamore, and Remara can settle in as little as 3–5 business days, compared to weeks for traditional bank refinancing.
Yes, you typically need at least 25% equity after both mortgages. For example, if your property is worth $1M with a $500k first mortgage, you have $500k equity and could access up to $250k via second mortgage.
A caveat or second charge is registered on the property title, so it’s visible. However, many second mortgage lenders don’t require first mortgagee consent.
Rates typically range from 11% to 18% p.a. depending on the lender, LVR, and loan term. While higher than first mortgages, the speed and flexibility often justify the cost for short-term needs.
