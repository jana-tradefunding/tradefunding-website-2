Frequently asked questions.

What is R&D funding?

R&D funding (also called R&D Tax Incentive financing) allows you to borrow against your expected R&D tax offset from the ATO before your claim is settled. Specialist lenders advance 70–85% of your estimated refund, giving you immediate cash flow to continue innovation while you wait for the ATO to process your claim.

How much can I borrow against my R&D Tax Incentive?

Most R&D funding providers advance 70–85% of your estimated R&D tax offset. The exact amount depends on your claim history, the quality of your R&D documentation, and the lender's assessment of ATO approval likelihood. Your Compare Report shows what each lender offers.

Who is eligible for R&D funding in Australia?

To qualify, your company must be registered for the R&D Tax Incentive with AusIndustry, have lodged at least one prior R&D claim, generate revenue of $1M+ per annum, and conduct eligible R&D activities in Australia. Some lenders also require your R&D tax advisor to confirm the claim.

What does R&D funding cost?

Costs typically range from 10–18% p.a. depending on the lender, your claim size, and risk profile. While this is higher than traditional lending, R&D funding is specialised and often non-recourse, meaning if the ATO reduces your claim, you may not owe the difference.

What happens if the ATO queries or delays my claim?

If the ATO queries or delays processing your claim, the term of the advance may extend and accrue additional cost. Your Compare Report flags which matched lenders offer non-recourse protection against this risk.
