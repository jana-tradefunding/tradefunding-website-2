3. Services We Provide

We provide credit assistance in relation to business lending products. We compare products from 70+ Australian lenders and provide personalised Compare Reports using our proprietary S.T.A.R. (Security, Term, Amount, Rate) scoring framework.

Our services include:

  • Comparing lending products across our panel of 70+ lenders
  • Providing personalised indicative offers via your Compare Report
  • Guiding you through your options using our S.T.A.R. scoring framework
  • Facilitating your chosen credit application through to settlement

4. How We Are Paid

We may receive commissions, referral fees, or trail commissions from Product Providers when you obtain a credit product through our services. These fees are paid by the Product Provider, not by you. The amount varies depending on the product and provider.

In some cases, we may charge a success fee. A success fee is only payable when you successfully access the funding you were seeking. This fee reflects the breadth of our service. We work across a broad base of 70+ lenders and product types to bring you the most suitable options for your specific circumstances, and in some cases we are not remunerated by, or fully covered by, the lender for our services.

We are fully transparent about fees in our process. Your Compare Report will clearly highlight which options include a success fee and which do not, so you can make an informed decision before proceeding.

Our Compare Report and guided walkthrough are provided free of charge. You are welcome to ask us about our remuneration in relation to any specific product at any time.

6. Complaints Handling

We take complaints seriously and are committed to resolving them fairly and promptly. If you have a concern about our services, please follow these steps:

Step 1 - Contact Us

In the first instance, please contact us directly:

  • Email: support@tradefunding.com.au
  • Phone: 1300 161 641

We will acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours of receipt.

Step 2 - Investigation

We aim to resolve all complaints within 30 calendar days. We will keep you informed of our progress throughout the investigation and provide you with a written response outlining our findings and any proposed resolution.

Step 3 - External Resolution

If you are not satisfied with our response, or if we are unable to resolve your complaint within 30 calendar days, you have the right to refer your complaint to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) - our external dispute resolution scheme.

Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)

  • Address: GPO Box 3, Melbourne VIC 3001
  • Phone: 1800 931 678
  • Email: info@afca.org.au

AFCA provides fair, independent, and free dispute resolution for financial services complaints. AFCA's service is free to consumers.
