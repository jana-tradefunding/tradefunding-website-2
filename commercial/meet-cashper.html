Meet Cashper

"Yes, I'm a Ghost.
No, That's Not Weird."

You're probably wondering why a lending intelligence platform has a ghost. Fair question. The marketing team thought I should explain myself, so here I am. Fully transparent (literally) and ready to clear things up.

Who (or What) is Cashper?

I'm Cashper, Trade Funding's resident ghost and voice of transparency. No tricks, no gimmicks, just straight answers about business finance.

The team chose me because finance can feel complicated, confusing, and, let's be honest, a little intimidating. My job is to make it clearer, make it simpler, and make sure you see through the noise to get the funding that fits your business.

A ghost might seem like a weird choice, but think about it:

I'm transparent. No hidden fees, no sneaky clauses, just clarity.

I help you see through the confusion. Comparing lenders shouldn't feel like a guessing game.

Finance shouldn't be scary. I make sure it isn't.

And no, I don't haunt offices. That would be unprofessional.

What Cashper Actually Does

Behind the friendly face, I'm an AI-powered lending intelligence engine. Here's how I build your personalised Compare Report:

1

Lending Profile

I absorb your business data and build a full lending profile: revenue, industry, trading history, security position.

2

Provider Matrix

Your profile is matched against 70+ lenders AND products based on your purpose of funds and business characteristics.

3

Credit Assessment

Individual credit engines, one for each lender, assess your creditworthiness against that lender's specific criteria.

4

Indicative Offers

Each engine produces personalised indicative terms. Not just "you might qualify", but actual estimated pricing.

5

S.T.A.R. Scoring

Offers are scored using our S.T.A.R. framework: the black and white facts about what each lender is offering, and what they want.

6

Guided Execution

Our team walks you through the report, helps you choose, and runs the loan through to settlement.

Everything You Didn't Know You Needed to Ask About Me

Why a ghost?

Because finance shouldn't be scary. Trade Funding believes business owners should know exactly what they're signing up for. No mystery, no confusion, just clear comparisons and real choices.

Are you a lender?

Nope. Lenders provide businesses with the funding they need, but not every option is the right fit for everyone. Trade Funding exists to help businesses compare their options confidently so they can choose the right lender for their needs.

What do you actually do?

I make sure Trade Funding sticks to its promise of clarity, honesty, and no tricks. I don't push products or play favourites. Behind the scenes, I'm also the AI engine that pre-qualifies your business against 70+ lenders and generates your S.T.A.R. scored Compare Report.

What's your deal with transparency?

It's kind of my thing. You can't get much more transparent than a ghost. Business owners deserve clear finance options, no hidden fees, and straight-up answers. If something doesn't add up, I call it out.

Can I trust you?

Well, I can't hold a pen, so I'm definitely not signing you up for anything. Trade Funding doesn't do pushy sales tactics, and neither do I. You get the facts, you make the decisions. Simple.

Do you have a last name?

Nope. Just Cashper. Like Beyoncé.
