Common questions

The questions every business asks before they register.

How much does Trade Funding cost my business?

Nothing. There are no setup fees, monthly fees or per-transaction fees. The lender pays Trade Funding when a customer's deal completes, the same way any commercial finance broker is paid. You receive 100% of the invoice value, settled directly to your bank, like any other invoice.

Do my customers pay more if they use Trade Funding?

Customers pay the lender's normal commercial finance rate. There's no surcharge from us. Because we compare 70+ lenders for each transaction, customers typically receive a better rate than they would through a single-lender program or a BNPL provider.

How long does it take to get set up?

Registration takes around five minutes. Full onboarding usually completes within the same week. We brand a QR code with your business identity, place it on your quote and invoice templates, and walk your team through the customer flow. Your dedicated growth manager handles the rollout.

Which lenders do you compare?

Over 70 Australian lenders across major banks, non-bank lenders, asset financiers, fintech credit providers and private credit funds. We match each customer transaction against relevant credit policies and product types to find the strongest fit available.

What kinds of businesses can register?

Any Australian business that sells to other businesses on quote or invoice terms. We work with vendors of equipment, machinery, vehicles, materials, stock, inventory, software subscriptions and professional services. There's no minimum transaction size, but most deals fall between $10,000 and $5,000,000.

Is there any credit risk on my balance sheet?

No. The customer's debt sits with the lender, not your business. You're paid in full at settlement, the same as any standard invoice. There's no recourse, no chargeback exposure, and no receivables sitting on your books.

What if the customer can't get approved?

Because we scan our whole panel, our approval rate is materially higher than any single-lender program. If a customer doesn't qualify with the best-fit lender, we work through the alternatives. If no lender on the panel can support the deal, the customer is told clearly and you continue your conversation with them as you normally would. You're never blocked from selling.

Is Trade Funding regulated?

Yes. Trade Funding Pty Ltd holds Australian Credit Licence 387856, is a member of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), is accredited with the Commercial Asset Finance Brokers Association (CAFBA), and is a member of Fintech Australia.
