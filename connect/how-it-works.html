How Connect Works

From quote to funded, without the back-and-forth.

A free, branded QR code on every quote and invoice gives your customer transparent finance options at the exact moment they're deciding whether to buy.

  1. 1

    You register and get your QR code

    Registration takes around five minutes. We brand a QR code with your business identity and place it on your quote and invoice templates.

  2. 2

    Your customer scans it on their quote

    Instead of one finance option (which might not be the best fit) or none, they see transparent options compared across 70+ lenders - non-intrusive, and entirely their choice.

  3. 3

    The customer is approved

    The finance sits with the lender, not your business - no recourse, no chargeback exposure, no receivables on your books.

  4. 4

    You get paid out

    You're paid in full at settlement, the same as any standard invoice - the sale moves forward instead of stalling on a financing question.
