FAQs

Connect frequently asked questions.

Answers for vendors and customers alike.

Do my customers pay more if they use Trade Funding?

Customers pay the lender's normal commercial finance rate, no surcharge from us. Because we compare 70+ lenders for each transaction, customers typically receive a better rate than they would through a single-lender finance program.

How long does it take to get set up?

Registration takes around five minutes. Full onboarding usually completes within the same week. We brand a QR code with your business identity, place it on your quote and invoice templates, and walk your team through the customer flow.

Which lenders do you compare?

Over 70 Australian lenders across major banks, non-bank lenders, asset financiers, fintech credit providers and private credit funds.

What kinds of businesses can use Trade Funding Connect?

Any Australian business that sells to other businesses on quote or invoice terms - vendors of equipment, machinery, vehicles, materials, stock, inventory, software subscriptions, and professional services.

Is there any credit risk on my balance sheet?

No. The customer's debt sits with the lender, not your business. You are paid in full at settlement, the same as any standard invoice - no recourse, no chargeback exposure, no receivables on your books.

What happens if a customer defaults?

The finance agreement is between the customer and the lender. Your business has already been paid out at settlement, so a later default does not create recourse back to you.

Is Trade Funding regulated?

Yes. Trade Funding Pty Ltd holds Australian Credit Licence 387856, is a member of the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA), is accredited with the Commercial Asset Finance Brokers Association (CAFBA), and is a member of Fintech Australia.
