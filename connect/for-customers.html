For Customers

Financing your purchase, compared and transparent.

Scanned a Trade Funding Connect QR code on a quote or invoice? Here's what it means for you.

What is point-of-sale finance?

Instead of your vendor offering just one finance option (or none), Trade Funding compares options from 70+ Australian lenders for your specific purchase - so you see what's actually available before committing.

What does it cost me?

You pay the lender's normal commercial finance rate - no surcharge from Trade Funding. Comparing across 70+ lenders typically finds a better rate than a single-lender finance program.

Am I eligible?

Eligibility depends on the lender and the transaction - scanning the QR code and comparing options doesn't commit you to anything or affect your credit file. See our FAQs for more detail, or ask the business that gave you the quote.
