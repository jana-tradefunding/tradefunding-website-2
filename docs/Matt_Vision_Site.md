# Matt's Vision — Consolidated

> **This document supersedes any prior branding documents.** Where anything here conflicts with earlier tokens/branding files (e.g. `tokens.md`), this brief governs.

## Design corrections (supersede prior branding docs)
- **No icons, anywhere — one exemption.** The generic stock-style icons on the channel cards (bank, handshake, house) clash with a premium fintech look and cheapen it — per Matt's note. Ban icons entirely across the site, not just the homepage channel cards. Represent categories/channels through typography, layout, and colour instead of iconography. **The sole exemption is the home icon in the channel toggle** (the Home / Commercial / Connect / Personal and Property switcher shown on the three sub-channel pages) — that icon stays.
- **Dial back font weight.** Current headline weight reads too bold/thick. If a heading is set to `font-black` or `font-extrabold`, step it down to `font-bold` or `font-semibold`.
- **Typeface: DM Sans only, site-wide.** Drop the Work Sans/DM Sans split entirely — headings and body copy both use **DM Sans**. This overrides the earlier tokens.md pairing (Work Sans for headings, DM Sans for body). DM Sans has enough character at a lighter weight to command the page without a second typeface or maximum boldness.

## The theme, in one line
Super minimal, super modern. "Less is more." Square and Prospa are the reference points — lots of white space, restrained type, almost no filler sentences, everything doing visual work rather than explaining itself in paragraphs. Matt has flagged more than once that a page shouldn't burn real estate on a sentence when a strong design element could say the same thing faster (his exact frustration: too much space given to "a simple statement... not this much real estate for sentence only").

The aesthetic direction:
- Clean, crisp, confident — not corporate-dense.
- Bold, short headline statements over long copy blocks.
- One master brand statement holding the whole site together, with the three channels reading as **facets of one brand**, not three separate businesses bolted together.
- Design carries the "wow" (e.g. showing rather than telling that Trade Funding can compare 100+ products against a business's profile), copy is tight and confirms it.
- Consistent visual system across all three pillars — same card style, same icon language, one accent colour per pillar inside a shared system — so it reads as one brand family, not three logos glued together.

## Who the customer is
All three channels are the **same audience — business owners** — just caught at different funding moments:
- **Trade Funding (Commercial)** — the business owner needing capital for the business itself. This is the flagship: biggest revenue, most SEO equity, gets the primary hero real estate.
- **Trade Funding Connect** — not the business owner directly, but their customers at point-of-sale; the business owner is the one adopting Connect to help *their* customers say yes and get paid faster.
- **Trade Funding Personal & Property** — the business owner again, but wearing their personal hat: their home, investment property, or personal finance needs, separate from the business itself.

Matt's framing: don't present these as three competing brands. Present one masthead ("Business capital, from every angle" / "Wherever you need capital, we've built a way to get it"), then let the three pillars answer "which angle applies to me."

## How that plays out structurally (context only, not the focus)

**Nav bar — universal across all four pages:**
- Three items only, always: **Products, Compare, About.**
- "Products" is context-aware — it changes based on which page you're on. On Commercial, it shows Commercial's products. On Connect, Connect's. On Personal & Property, that channel's categories. Same pattern everywhere — the label stays fixed, the content underneath adapts to context.
- Hovering "Products" reveals bold sub-headings tied to the active channel, e.g.:
  - **"FUND MY BUSINESS"** — Compare options (Commercial)
  - **"SUPPORT CLIENT PAYMENTS"** — the funding market at point-of-sale (Connect)
  - **"FUNDING OWNERS RUNNING BUSINESS"** — for their personal and property needs (Personal & Property)
- "Choice" is being renamed **"Compare."** Hovering "Compare" shows "Which Option is Best" — clicking through opens a dedicated Compare page explaining the matching engine (Cashper), 70+ lenders, 100+ products, and showcasing the Compare Report. From there the visitor self-selects: funding for your business / for you personally / to support your customers — each routing to the relevant sub-site.

**Channel toggle — separate from the nav bar:**
- A toggle switches between the four pages: a home icon (Home), then **Commercial**, **Connect**, **Personal and Property**.
- Present on Commercial, Connect, and Personal & Property pages — **not shown on the homepage.**
- Needs its own visual identity: border and shadow so it sits distinctly above/apart from the nav bar rather than blending into it.
- Switching needs to feel smooth — both the toggle's own state change and the page transition when you switch channels. This applies across all four pages, not just via the toggle.

**Homepage-specific:**
- The supporting "we can compare 100+ products against your profile" statement was cut from the homepage by accident in the last version and needs to come back — Matt's point is this is about transparency, removing complexity, and empowering good decisions, not just a slogan.
- The short explainer text that used to sit near the sticky channel nav should move: it disappears from the homepage as standalone copy and instead appears inside the hover state of each large framed channel box, in a shorter form.

Matt's own read on progress: navigation and structure are "good, ok" — the outstanding work is content, phrasing and design polish, plus fleshing out Compare and the About sub-navigation once the whole site is indexed.

# Page Briefs — Content Basis for Copy

## 1. Home
**Job of the page:** carry the master brand statement and route people to the right channel — minimal, confident, mostly design-led.

- Hero: master statement (e.g. *"Business capital, from every angle"* or *"Wherever you need capital, we've built a way to get it"*) + the core Trade Funding (Commercial) CTA, since it's the biggest revenue driver and carries the most SEO recognition.
- Below the fold: "Three ways we help business owners," introducing Connect and Personal & Property as extensions — one-line explainer each, "learn more," not equal real estate to Commercial.
- "Products" nav → mega-menu with the three bold facet headings above.
- Large framed channel boxes (Commercial / Connect / Personal & Property) — hover reveals a short supporting line per channel, e.g. Connect's: *"Help your customers say yes, and get paid on the spot."*
- A strong visual/design moment (not a paragraph) making the point: "Can you compare 100+ products against your business's profile and performance? We can." This needs a design solution, not more copy.
- Footer carries all three channels as equal peers — this is where full parity is fine, since by the footer the visitor already understands the family.
- Tightened family taglines to keep rhythm consistent (verb + who + what, each under 8 words):
  - Trade Funding — *Compare, choose, and access business capital — with full transparency.*
  - Trade Funding Connect — *Fund your customers. Get paid faster.*
  - Trade Funding Personal & Property — *Personal and property funding, for the business owner behind the business.*

## 2. Commercial
**Job of the page:** the flagship channel — most revenue, most SEO equity, primary CTA target from the homepage hero.

- Owns the "Compare" experience: Compare page explains the Cashper matching engine, 70+ lenders, 100+ products, and showcases the S.T.A.R. Compare Report (ranked on Security, Term, Amount, Rate).
- On the Compare page, asks the visitor what they want to compare funding for — their business, themselves personally, or to support their own customers — each option linking through to the relevant sub-site (Commercial / Personal & Property / Connect).
- ACL and AFCA membership remain the core trust/proof credentials.
- Tagline: *Compare, choose, and access business capital — with full transparency.*

## 3. Connect
**Job of the page:** solve low brand recognition contextually, not by grabbing more nav space.

- Positioning: *Fund your customers. Get paid faster.*
- Needs a supporting line wherever it's introduced (homepage hover, mega-menu) to build recognition through repetition and context rather than nav prominence — e.g. *"Help your customers say yes, and get paid on the spot."*
- Sits as a peer to Commercial and Personal & Property in the footer, and in the sticky in-site nav once a visitor is inside any sub-site.
