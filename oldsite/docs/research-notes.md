## Locked messaging direction (applies to every page below — plan.md §5.0)

- Lead with *why* (personalized, qualified options), not *how* (the matching mechanism).
- Avoid "compare report" / "score" language without explanation.
- Reinforce the one-service, three-channel story: business gets funding options (Commercial), the business's customers get funded at point of sale (Connect), the people who run the business get personal/property funding (Personal & Property).
- Copy-style guardrails from hit-list items 31–32 (tone/terminology/formatting rules) apply alongside the above — cross-reference `Team Comments.md` / hit list at drafting time if specifics are needed beyond what's captured here.

## SEO guardrails that apply site-wide (from SEO_Audit_Report.md — apply to every new/rewritten page, not just one section)

- **Every new page needs a permanent, canonical, indexable URL** — never a filtered/listing/faceted view. This is the audit's #1 structural finding (only 15 of 28 crawled URLs are indexable; the rest are non-indexable facets or redirect chains).
- **Meta title + description must lead with a specific benefit and a number** (rate, speed, amount, timeframe) — the audit found pages ranking well (position 3–4) but converting under 1% of impressions, which points squarely at weak/generic snippets, not a ranking problem.
- **Above-the-fold trust signals** (lender/accreditation logos, testimonials, indicative approval timeframes) are called out as a near-term priority across top-traffic and campaign pages.
- **Mobile experience matters disproportionately**: mobile impressions nearly match desktop but convert at a lower CTR — keep forms short, titles un-truncated, tap targets sized.
- **Every paid/organic campaign and CTA must map to one canonical landing URL**, never a listing/catalogue view — relevant to any page that will receive campaign traffic (Business Loans Hub, Broker Portal, Connect, Personal & Property product pages).
- **Non-brand SME finance intent is the single largest opportunity** in the account (thousands of impressions, near-zero clicks on non-brand terms) — new pages should be written to capture specific non-brand queries where relevant, not just brand traffic. Relevant queries flagged in the audit: "business funding sydney" (local/commercial intent), "trade finance loan" (dedicated product page needed), "trade finance brokers" (partner/broker-facing page — directly relevant to Broker Portal), "trade finance companies" (comparison/category framing).
- **Simplify lead-capture forms** to minimum viable fields wherever a page ends in a form/CTA.
