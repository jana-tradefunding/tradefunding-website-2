Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30th March 2025 | Trade Funding Pty Ltd (ABN 28 647 543 084)

This Privacy Policy explains how Trade Funding Pty Ltd (ABN 28 647 543 084) collects, maintains, uses, and discloses your Personal Information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). By accessing our website or submitting an enquiry or application, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. We encourage you to read this document carefully. If you have questions or concerns, please contact us using the details provided at the end of this policy.

1. Key Definitions

The following definitions apply throughout this Privacy Policy:

  • Trade Funding / we / us / our means Trade Funding Pty Ltd (ABN 28 647 543 084), Australian Credit Licence 387856.
  • Credit Information means information about an individual that is collected in connection with a credit application or credit account, including repayment history, credit liability information, default information, and court proceedings information, as defined under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).
  • Personal Information means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether the information or opinion is true or not, and whether the information or opinion is recorded in a material form or not.
  • Product Provider means any bank, non-bank lender, finance company, credit provider, or other financial institution with whom we may introduce or refer you in connection with a credit product or financial service.
  • Customer means any individual or business entity that has engaged Trade Funding to assist in sourcing or arranging a financial product or service.
  • Visitor means any individual who accesses or browses our website at tradefunding.com.au without necessarily submitting an enquiry or application.
  • Applicant means any individual or business entity that has submitted an enquiry or formal application through our website or directly to our team.
  • Sensitive Information means a sub-set of Personal Information that is given a higher level of protection under the APPs, including health information, information about racial or ethnic origin, and information about criminal record. We do not ordinarily collect Sensitive Information unless required to verify your identity or comply with a legal obligation, in which case we will seek your explicit consent.

2. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect Personal Information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services and to introduce you to suitable Product Providers. The types of information we collect may include, but are not limited to:

Personal and Contact Details

  • Full name, date of birth, and residential address
  • Email address and telephone number(s)
  • Identity documents such as a driver's licence, passport, or Medicare card (for identity verification purposes)

Business Information

  • Australian Business Number (ABN) or Australian Company Number (ACN)
  • Registered and trading business name(s)
  • Business structure (sole trader, partnership, company, trust)
  • Industry type and time in business

Financial Information

  • Annual or monthly revenue, turnover, and net profit
  • Payment history and existing credit obligations
  • Business bank statements (typically 3–6 months)
  • Tax returns, BAS statements, and other financial statements
  • Details of existing loans, assets, and liabilities

Credit Information

  • Credit reports and credit scores obtained from credit reporting bodies
  • Repayment history information in respect of credit accounts
  • Information about credit enquiries, defaults, or court judgements

Website Interaction Data

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs
  • Cookie identifiers and analytics data (see Section 7)

We collect Personal Information directly from you wherever practicable - for example, when you complete an online enquiry form, call our team, or email us. We may also collect information from third parties such as credit reporting bodies, identity verification services, and publicly available sources where permitted by law.

3. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use your Personal Information only for the purposes for which it was collected, or as otherwise permitted by the APPs. These purposes include:

  • Establishing a relationship: To verify your identity, set up your customer or applicant profile, and administer our engagement with you.
  • Providing our services: To assess your financing needs, prepare a personalised Compare Report, and identify suitable lending options from our panel of Product Providers.
  • Sourcing credit: To submit applications or referrals to one or more Product Providers on your behalf, and to facilitate the assessment and approval of credit products.
  • Introductions to Product Providers: To share your information with Product Providers for the purpose of obtaining credit quotes, indicative approvals, or formal credit assessments.
  • Credit checks and analytics: To conduct or facilitate credit checks with credit reporting bodies, and to assess your credit profile to determine suitable product options.
  • Email and marketing communications: To send you information about our services, product updates, and educational content relevant to business finance, where you have consented to receive such communications. You may opt out at any time.
  • Regulatory and legal obligations: To respond to requests from regulatory bodies, law enforcement agencies, or courts, and to comply with our obligations under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth), the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth), and other applicable laws.
  • Improving our services: To analyse website usage, monitor performance, and enhance the quality of our services and customer experience.

4. Disclosure of Personal Information

We may disclose your Personal Information to the following categories of third parties, to the extent necessary to provide our services or comply with our legal obligations:

  • Product Providers: Banks, non-bank lenders, and other credit providers on our panel, to whom we submit applications or referrals on your behalf.
  • Guarantors and related parties: Where a guarantor is required as part of a credit application, we may share relevant information with that guarantor and obtain their consent to proceed.
  • Identity verification providers: Third-party services that assist us in verifying your identity in accordance with our AML/CTF obligations.
  • Finance consultants and brokers: Accredited professionals within our network who may assist in processing your application or providing specialist advice.
  • Associates and related entities: Related bodies corporate of Trade Funding Pty Ltd, where necessary to facilitate the provision of services.
  • Contractors and service providers: Third-party technology, IT infrastructure, marketing, and professional services providers engaged by us to support business operations, subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulatory and government bodies: ASIC, AUSTRAC, the Australian Taxation Office, and other government agencies where disclosure is required or authorised by law.
  • Credit reporting bodies: Equifax, Illion, Experian, and similar credit reporting bodies as permitted under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

We will not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Security of Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your Personal Information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. These steps include physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information held.

Our systems are protected by SSL/TLS encryption, and access to Personal Information is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis. We regularly review our security practices and update them in response to emerging risks.

Whilst we take all reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to us electronically, and any such transmission is at your own risk. If you have reason to believe that your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us immediately.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse site usage, and deliver relevant content. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by your browser when you visit our site.

Types of cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly, including session management and form functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymised data about how visitors interact with our website, including pages visited, time on site, and traffic sources. This data helps us improve our content and user experience.
  • Preference cookies: Used to remember your consent choices and site preferences across sessions.

Third-party services such as Google Analytics may also set their own cookies on your device in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not control these cookies and encourage you to review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Instructions for managing cookies are available in your browser's help documentation.

7. Cross-Border Disclosure of Personal Information

Some of our third-party service providers and technology infrastructure are located outside Australia. As a result, your Personal Information may be stored on, or accessible from, servers located in the United States of America and the Philippines, as well as other jurisdictions from time to time.

Where we disclose Personal Information to overseas recipients, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient does not breach the APPs in relation to that information. However, by using our website and providing us with your Personal Information, you consent to your information being held and processed in these jurisdictions, and acknowledge that Australian privacy law may not apply in full to the handling of your information by overseas recipients.

8. Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to the Personal Information we hold about you, and to request that we correct any information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading.

To make an access or correction request, please contact us in writing using the details set out in Section 11 below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe and, in any event, within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before granting access.

In some circumstances, we may decline an access or correction request where permitted by the APPs - for example, where providing access would unreasonably impact the privacy of another individual, or where the information relates to a legal matter. If we decline your request, we will provide written reasons and advise you of the steps you may take to escalate the matter.

9. Complaints

If you believe we have breached your privacy or handled your Personal Information in a manner inconsistent with this Privacy Policy or the APPs, we encourage you to contact us in the first instance so that we can attempt to resolve your concern promptly.

Please submit your complaint in writing to support@tradefunding.com.au. We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 5 business days and aim to provide a substantive response within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, or if we fail to respond within the timeframe stated above, you may escalate your complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):

  • Website: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
  • Post: GPO Box 5218, Sydney NSW 2001

We reserve the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in our practices, legal obligations, or business operations. The revised policy will be effective upon posting to our website. We recommend that you review this page periodically to stay informed of any changes.

Where changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, which may include posting a notice on our website or contacting you directly where we have your contact details.
